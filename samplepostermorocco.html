Visit Morocco!

"Desert, mountains, ocean — and the best mint tea in the world!"
★★★★★
Places to Visit
  • Marrakech – the Red City
  • Fez – ancient medina
  • Chefchaouen – blue city!
  • Sahara Desert
  • Casablanca – biggest city
  • Essaouira – ocean town
Food to Try
  • Tagine – slow-cooked stew
  • Couscous – every Friday!
  • Pastilla – sweet + savory pie
  • Harira – tomato soup
  • Msemen – flat bread
  • Mint tea (always!)
Travel Tips

Dress modestly — cover shoulders and knees.

Bargain in the souks — it's normal!

Always say shukran (thank you).

Best time: March–May or Sept–Nov.

5-Day Itinerary
DAY 1Arrive Marrakech. Walk Djemaa el-Fna square. Eat tagine and drink mint tea!
DAY 2Marrakech: Majorelle Garden + souk shopping. Bargain for a carpet!
DAY 3Bus to Fez. Tour the ancient medina and the famous leather tanneries.
DAY 4Drive to Sahara Desert. Camel ride at sunset. Sleep under the stars in a desert camp!
DAY 5Visit Chefchaouen — the famous blue city! Take photos. Fly home from Casablanca.

Getting Around
  • Train between big cities
  • CTM bus (comfortable)
  • Grand taxi for short trips
  • Walk inside the medinas
  • Rent a car for the desert
Where to Stay
  • Riad – traditional house!
  • Stay inside the old medina
  • Desert camp for one night
  • Riads in Fez are cheap!
  • Book early in spring
Interesting Facts!
Morocco has the oldest university in the world — founded in 859 AD!

It has coasts on BOTH the Atlantic Ocean AND the Mediterranean Sea.

Morocco was the first country to recognize American independence in 1777!
