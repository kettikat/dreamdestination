Destination

Your group will plan a trip together, create a poster, and present it to the class — all in English.

⏱ 60–90 minutes 👥 Groups of 3–4 🗣 Speak English the whole time
Your mission: Work with your group to plan a 5-day trip to one country represented in your group. Decide where to go, what to do, what to eat, and how to travel. Then make a poster and give a short presentation to the class.

The situation: You and your classmates work together at a travel agency. A customer wants a 5-day trip to a country none of them have visited before. Your group must choose one destination country (it must be the home country of someone in your group), plan the full trip, design a poster to advertise it, and present it to the customer — the class.
1
Choose Your Destination & Assign Roles
Discuss and decide together
⏱ ~10 min
  • →Introduce yourselves. Tell the group your name and home country.
  • →Discuss: Which country should we plan the trip to? Everyone gives an opinion. Vote if you need to. The chosen country should be someone's home country so that person can be the expert.
  • →Assign a role to each person (see below). If your group has 3 people, one person takes two roles.
🗺 The Destination Expert
You know the most about this country. Help your group understand the places, food, and culture. Answer their questions.
📅 The Itinerary Planner
You organize the 5-day schedule. Decide what happens on each day. Make sure the plan is realistic and fun.
🎨 The Poster Designer
You lead the creation of the poster. Decide what to write, draw, or include. Make it look attractive.
🎤 The Presenter
You lead the presentation to the class. Practice what to say. Make sure everyone in the group speaks at least once.
Phrases for deciding
"I think we should go to ___." "What does everyone think?" "I agree / I disagree because…" "Let's vote." "Can you explain that?"
2
Plan the Trip
The Destination Expert answers questions — everyone else asks and takes notes
⏱ ~20 min
  • →Together, discuss and decide on the details below. The Itinerary Planner writes down the group's decisions on the back of this sheet or on a separate paper.
  • →The Destination Expert should share real information — places they know, food they love, things tourists actually do.
  • →Everyone should ask questions and give opinions. Don't let one person decide everything!
🏙 Cities & Places Which city do you fly into? What are the best places to visit?
🍜 Food & Drinks What must tourists try? Any restaurants or markets?
🏨 Where to Stay Hotel, hostel, or with a family? In the city or outside?
🚌 Getting Around Train, bus, taxi, walking? How do people travel there?
🎉 Culture & Tips What should tourists know? Any customs or polite behavior?
📅 Day-by-Day Plan What happens on Day 1, Day 2… Day 5?
Phrases for planning
"What do you recommend?" "How long does that take?" "Is that expensive?" "What about Day 3 — what should we do?" "That's a great idea!" "I'm not sure. Maybe…"
3
Make Your Poster
Use the paper your teacher left — everyone contributes
⏱ ~20 min
  • →The Poster Designer leads, but everyone helps write and draw. Divide the sections between group members so you all work at the same time.
  • →Write in English. Use large, clear writing so the class can read it from a distance.
  • →While you work, keep talking — read things aloud, ask "how do you spell ___?", give opinions on what looks good.

📌 Must Include

  • Country name & flag (drawn)
  • Title: "Visit [Country]!"
  • 3–5 places to visit
  • 2–3 foods to try
  • The 5-day itinerary (Day 1–5)
  • One travel tip for tourists

⭐ Nice to Add

  • Simple drawings or maps
  • A slogan or catchphrase
  • Words in the local language
  • Weather or best time to visit
  • An interesting fact
  • A rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Phrases for collaborating
"You write this part, I'll draw." "How do you spell ___?" "Does this look good?" "Can you make that bigger?" "I think we should add ___."
4
Prepare & Practice Your Presentation
Every group member speaks during the presentation
⏱ ~10 min
  • →The Presenter organizes who says what. Divide the poster into sections and assign each section to a person.
  • →Practice your part out loud — don't just read it silently. Speak to your group as if they are the audience.
  • →Give each other feedback: "Speak a little louder." / "That was clear!" / "Can you explain that part more?"
1
Introduction (Presenter): "Hello everyone! We are the travel agents for [Country]. Today we want to invite you on an amazing trip…"
2
The Destination (Expert): Tell the class about the country — where it is, what it's famous for, what the culture is like.
3
The Itinerary (Planner): Walk through the 5-day plan. Explain what tourists will do each day.
4
Closing (Presenter): "We hope you will visit [Country]! Do you have any questions?"
Phrases for presenting
"Our destination is…" "We recommend…" "On Day 1, tourists will…" "One important thing to know is…" "Any questions?"
Remember: Your teacher will ask you about your project when she returns. Be ready to explain your choices — why you picked that country, what you enjoyed planning, and what was difficult. Speak English the entire time. 🗣
