📍 Places to Visit
  • Palawan – most beautiful island
  • Boracay – famous white beach
  • Bohol – Chocolate Hills!
  • Manila – the capital city
  • Siargao – surfing paradise
  • Vigan – Spanish colonial town
🍽 Food to Try
  • Adobo – chicken in vinegar + soy
  • Sinigang – sour soup
  • Lechon – whole roast pig!
  • Halo-halo – colorful cold dessert
  • Balut – adventurous street food
  • Mango – best in the world! 🥭
🚌 Transport & Stay
  • Ride a Jeepney (fun & cheap!)
  • Tricycle for short trips
  • Ferries between islands
  • Domestic flights affordable
  • Beach resorts in Palawan
  • Budget hotels in Manila
📅 5-Day Itinerary
1
Arrive Manila. Try halo-halo. Visit Intramuros old city.
2
Fly to Palawan. Island hopping. Snorkel in crystal water!
3
Underground River tour. Eat lechon dinner on the beach. 🌅
4
Fly to Bohol. See Chocolate Hills. Meet a tarsier! 🐒
5
Relax on Alona Beach. Buy souvenirs. Fly home. 😊
💡 Travel Tips

Smile a lot — Filipinos are very friendly!
Say "po" and "opo" to show respect.
Bring sunscreen — very hot and sunny!
Best time: November–April (dry season)

🌟 Interesting Facts

The Philippines has 7,641 islands!
Basketball is the #1 sport 🏀
One of the biggest English-speaking countries in Asia.
Home to 700+ species of birds! 🦜
