📍 Places to Visit
  • Cartagena – colonial city by the sea
  • Medellín – the city of eternal spring
  • Bogotá – capital, museums, markets
  • Coffee Region (Eje Cafetero)
  • Tayrona National Park
🍽 Food to Try
  • Arepa – corn bread (breakfast!)
  • Bandeja Paisa – big rice plate
  • Ajiaco – chicken soup
  • Empanadas – fried pockets
  • Fresh tropical fruit juices!
🚌 Getting Around
  • Domestic flights are cheap
  • Medellín has a Metro + cable car!
  • Buses between cities
  • Taxis – always use the app (Uber/InDriver)
🏨 Where to Stay
  • Cartagena – hotel in the walled city
  • Medellín – El Poblado neighborhood
  • Hostels are very cheap and friendly
  • Airbnb options everywhere
📅 5-Day Itinerary
DAY 1Arrive Bogotá. Walk La Candelaria. Try ajiaco soup!
DAY 2Bogotá: Gold Museum + Monserrate mountain. Good views!
DAY 3Fly to Cartagena. Walk the walled city. Eat seafood dinner.
DAY 4Cartagena: beach + boat trip to Rosario Islands. 🏝
DAY 5Coffee Region: visit a coffee farm. Fly home. ☕
💡 Important Travel Tips
Learn a little Spanish – people love it!
Don't walk with your phone visible.
Use sunscreen – it's very sunny!
Always try the fresh fruit – it's amazing. 🍉
🌟 Interesting Facts
Colombia has 2 coastlines: Pacific & Caribbean!
It's the #2 producer of coffee in the world.
Home to 1,900 species of birds 🦜
Best time to visit: December–March (dry season)
¡Bienvenidos a Colombia!
"Welcome to Colombia!" — and don't forget to say ¡Provecho! before you eat (= enjoy your meal!)
